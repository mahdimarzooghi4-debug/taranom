## Why now

Sprint66 connected a real test-only support workflow but embedded its *member* panel in the *center* UI. Although only synthetic IDs and localhost were used, this was structurally misleading: two independent product actors should not share one page. The scope of Sprint67 is **actual compiled frontend separation with retained real backend/DB/browser behavior**. It does NOT simulate real member authentication, tenant authorization, clinical access or protected claims.

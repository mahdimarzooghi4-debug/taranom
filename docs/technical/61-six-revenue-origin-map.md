## Product gap / deliberate exclusions

Previous sprints establish membership-funding separation, member-specific credit period and source-sensitive bills. However, six **different operating-income origins** must not be accidentally collapsed into the member's individual wallet, double-counted across financial pools or have subscription fees invent a seventh source. The eligibility cohort contract (TRN-DEC-039), recognized net/realized financial basis, per-stream allocation percentage/timing, financial backing/authority, accounting treatment, tax and member amounts (TRN-DEC-021/022/023) are still unapproved. This sprint MUST NOT implement amounts, financial formulas or a policy evaluator for these undefined elements.

## New internal technical boundary

Pure `SyntheticSixRevenueEvidenceMap.Inspect` is a READ of only bounded untrusted `test:` tenant/operator/financial-period markers and `SyntheticRevenueOriginClaim` input fixtures; no DB, real accounts, patient data, signed issuer, bank, fund, insurer, provider, authority, financial action, revenue recognition, market entitlement or actual medical payment exists.

The exactly six versioned enum **SOURCE TYPES** are: TreatmentDevelopmentFundProfitShare; HealthDevelopmentFundProfitShare; FixedIncomeFundManagementFeeCashback; SupplementalInsuranceReferralCommission; HospitalInvoiceCommission; DirectTaranomIncome. `MembershipSubscription` is a typed **component only of DirectTaranomIncome**. No enum `MemberSubscriptionRevenueStream` exists. The map binds each claim to the same tenant/operator/period, nonzero request UUID, source event marker/revision, evidence marker, and four independent untrusted source/legal/accounting/custodian/anti-double-count review checks. Malformed/real IDs, unknown enum values, cross-scope claims and duplicate UUID fail closed with no read projection. An exact underlying source-event marker cited multiple times (even at different revisions, under one or several streams, or with different paper evidence) becomes `PotentialDoubleCountUnresolved`; **no inference of actual fraud or double revenue** is permitted. For every source lane, clearly distinguish `UnrepresentedInSuppliedEvidence` (NOT actual zero income), missing/rejected untrusted evidence, possible source reuse and unverified claimed presence. The output remains order-invariant and does not expose source IDs, amounts, rates, financial dates or actual member identities.

Regardless of whether all six categories have caller-claimed `Confirmed`, `FinancialRevenueRecognized=false`, `AllocationAmountCalculated=false`, `EqualShareDistributionExecuted=false`, `TreatmentWalletCredited=false`, `MembershipFeesBecameSeventhRevenueStream=false`. A positive `UnverifiedSixSourceClaimsOnly` is NOT genuine income, member eligibility, equal allocation authorization or budget sufficiency. Verification against an authoritative licensed financial ledger and independently approved accounting policy is outside this sprint.

## Why this slice

Both existing `ProofingCaseJournal` + `IdentityProofingReview` and `SyntheticSubscriptionPayerBoundary` had comprehensive separate unit gates, but *no read-only composition* to ensure payer claims could not be combined with a stale/replaced identity or accidentally start a waiting period. This is an **evidence checklist inspection**, NOT the approved missing business contracts for actual activation. It must never become an activation command or imply all conditions are legally decided.

## Domain

New `SyntheticMembershipActivationEvidenceGate.Inspect` reads a caller-provided `ProofingCaseSnapshot` and a caller-provided `SyntheticSubscriptionPayerInput` (requiring `InspectSubscriptionFundingEvidence` only). Each subject/tenant/case is a bounded opaque `test:` reference. The gate **replays each identity event through the existing `ProofingCaseJournal`**, validating exact case revisions, identity revisions and derived review, including corrections. It requires a latest VerifiedMatch observation at the current identity revision, preventing re-use of earlier proofing after a self-service correction. Binding to the requested person, source professional licensure and contract, lawful processing, explicit member terms, current official activation state and all other yet-to-be-determined conditions each retain **independent caller-claimed `EvidenceCheck`**. No verified identity provider exists. These claims are never a trustworthy attestation.

Funding uses the existing gate, pinning the exact requested member/tenant, individual wallet owner and beneficiary plus the requested capability; only `FundingEvidencePresentOnly` may be regarded as a positive **claim**. Sponsored membership neither confers identity nor allows a sponsor to activate membership or read/own the member's health/wallet rights. The result distinguishes malformed/stale history, pending re-verification, currently already-active domain boundary, funding-contract cross-scope and untrusted rejected/missing evidence. Even all claimed `Confirmed` result **only** in `ClaimedPrerequisitesPresentOnly` with structural revisions; read fields `OfficialActivationIssued` and `WaitingClockStarted` **always false**. No price, bank transaction, actual member identity, national identifier, default waiver, legal eligibility, autonomous decision engine, authority delegation, account state transition, waiting start, wallet accrual or payment API.

# TRN-TECH-059 — Synthetic pre-treatment estimate versus post-treatment bill source comparison

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Base:** Sprint49 PR #56, Draft/Open. **Accepted Business:** TRN-BIZ-050 (five legally separate source lanes of post-service billing), TRN-BIZ-051 (nonbinding five-source pre-treatment estimate), TRN-BIZ-052 (choice about exact estimate independent of legal consent/payment), TRN-BIZ-049 (insurer adjustments stay insurer controlled), TRN-BIZ-034/035 (emergency care never awaits cost; sponsor cannot access private care bills). Backlog TRN-EP-07/09/10/13.

## Product distinction
Before service, the member may encounter a clearly nonbinding five-part estimate. After service, the lawful source issuer must issue an **independent** bill; it cannot be treated as just a revision of the estimate. Sprint49 compared two estimates, while Sprint50 distinctly addresses an estimate vs post-service bill and avoids deriving invented overcharge, insurer entitlement or automatic credit/refund.

## Precisely bounded implementation
New `SyntheticEstimateBillComparison.Inspect` is INTERNAL PURE **TEST-ONLY** and accepts only caller-supplied synthetic documents and a `test:` *claimed correspondence* marker. The old preview schema **does not contain a treatment reference**: the marker is explicitly unverified and must never be used as a real treatment match or legal link. It first invokes Sprint49's `SyntheticCostChoiceContinuity.Inspect(preview, historicalChoice, preview)` to reconstruct the original estimate and replay all prior nonbinding member-choice commands. Then invokes the existing `SyntheticTreatmentBillingAssembler.Build` on all bill events with exact member, treatment, bill packet UUID and revision. Structurally malformed, forged fingerprint, incorrect issuer, corrupted history, wrong member/treatment/packet, missing claimed correspondence or unapproved sources fail closed. A source checksum is **not an attested legal issuer signature**.

For each of five matched *category labels only*, the projection returns a non-authorizing state: both unverified numeric **claims** same or different; preview missing or conflicted; bill source missing/withdrawn or disputed; claimed currencies differ. It never computes money, debt, a difference amount, exchange, benefits, suspected overcharge, eligibility, insurer decision or final price. It does NOT compare immutable preview source identifiers with legally independent bill source identifiers as if they were the same issuer. Pre-treatment uncertainty and post-treatment disputes carry no numeric liability. An actual claimed zero is distinct from a missing value. Response surfaces only typed states, unverified past choice, preview/bill IDs and revisions; `OfficialBillConfirmed`, `PaymentOrRefundAuthorized`, `WalletMutated`, `ClinicalConsentGranted` are **always false**. The existing synthetic cost PostgreSQL journal and insurer-dispute handoff remain unchanged.

## Member Web and checks
Clarify the new **three-step comparison** in existing Persian RTL `/member/billing`: first nonbinding pre-service estimate, then independent unverified after-service invoice source by lane, then legally governed dispute/review distinct from financial settlement. No real estimate, invoice, insurance, member, wallet balance, amount or responder exists; all displayed states unknown, actions disabled and emergency care unaffected. Four built Chromium cases verify no overcharge claim, no automatic refund, sponsor privacy, focus/history and absence of API/storage. xUnit adversarial matrix for five unknown/conflicted/disputed/withdrawn/different numeric statuses, source tampering, wrong subjects or packet, no link and no real financial decision.

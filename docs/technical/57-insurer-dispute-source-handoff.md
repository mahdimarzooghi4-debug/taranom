## Sprint48 new bounded evidence trail

`SyntheticInsuranceDisputeTrail` is an INTERNAL pure immutable .NET **synthetic-only** source-pinned paper trail, NOT insurer workflow or API. Opening requires BOTH independently supplied historical contexts: a re-playable `SyntheticSupportSnapshot` with exact `InsuranceCoverageDispute` topic and case/member, and a full `SyntheticTreatmentBillingAssembler.Build` result containing the exact **currently Disputed** `PrimaryInsurance` or `SupplementalInsurance` lane. The opening binds treatment, bill packet UUID, disputed event UUID, per-lane revision, channel `DirectLicensedInsurer` (no intermediary) or `LicensedIntermediary` (required distinct `test:` intermediary), and `test:` insurer label. **No contract, regulator or official coverage evidence is verified by these claimed labels.**

For every paper note, the gate rebuilds/replays the existing support journal and bill, and pins both the original support revision and the canonical order-invariant billing metadata fingerprint (unverified hash, NOT a trusted signature). Any change or supersession after the opening must be separately reviewed: this case **cannot silently rebind** to a new bill/disputed event or different member. Stale expected case revision, malformed inputs, different issuer lane (e.g. provider/credit), wrong original, cross-tenant analogue via case/member/treatment, repeated request UUID changed payload, absent original, and duplicate stages deny. The paper stages are: (1) unverified scoped issue, (2) claimed outbound forward *request*, (3) distinct claimed insurer *receipt*, and (4) claimed insurer response-*source note*. A receipt needs the exact forward UUID and separate unverified actor; a response requires receipt. **No stage confirms actual submission, receipt, coverage decision, adjustment, or reimbursement.** There is no amount, signed decision artifact or wallet mutation.

The read contract contains only case/member, disputed insurance lane, revision and booleans for **unverified notes**. It cannot express `Approved`, `Paid`, `Settled`, actual insurer decision or source-verified legal adjustment. The existing bill remains disputed and its older numeric claim masked; any real correction would require authentic insurer/provider/custodian source history, independent adjudication, and financial reconciliation not implemented here.

## New product distinction

Earlier `SyntheticCareCoordinationTrail` only keeps claimed nonclinical member request/coordinator/clinical-source *paper* notes, while `SyntheticPreventionParticipationGate` independently checks caller-claimed education/prevention/longitudinal-care participation prerequisites. A legitimate future member journey must be able to **inspect the continuity boundary** without treating an earlier coordinator contact, clinician paper attachment, paid membership or historic expression of interest as automatic enrollment, medical approval or research consent.

## Contract

`SyntheticCarePreventionContinuity.Inspect` is INTERNAL PURE and READ-ONLY, limited to `test:` fixtures. It **replays the original care journal through the existing reducer**, verifies every source event and original revision, exact expected current revision and case/member scope; it independently invokes the **existing** `SyntheticPreventionParticipationGate.Assess` for one of three tracks (education, nonclinical prevention coordination, clinician-led longitudinal care) and two permitted origins (member-requested or qualified clinician-proposed). An explicit `test:` claimed correspondence links the two for the synthetic comparison only: the underlying care trail contains NO tenant, clinical program, service owner or legally certified referral foreign key. It cannot prove the same actual encounter. A changed synthetic member/case, lost history, stale revision, malformed link, invalid proposer, no member choice or revoked/rejected source never becomes approved participation.

Outcomes separate: missing original care request, malformed/replayed care history, stale version, cross-member/case, invalid participation scope, member decline, missing/rejected claimed prerequisites, independent clinical decision or separate research consent, missing claimed clinical reference for continuing-care observation, and merely `ClaimedPrerequisitesPresentOnly`. **Even a positive category is NOT a member opt-in, content publication, actual clinical source, care plan, appointment, successful contact, ongoing monitoring, notification or license.** A coordinator contact and a clinical-source reference remain distinct **unverified counts**, never evidence of delivery. `ParticipationActivated`, `EducationPublished`, `ClinicalCareStartedOrChanged`, `ResearchOrAiConsentGranted`, `MedicalRecordDisclosed`, `AutomatedFollowUpScheduled` are false for ALL outcomes. No dates, cadence, risk score, automated triggers, thresholds, diagnosis, signed consent, user/health record or provider identifiers.

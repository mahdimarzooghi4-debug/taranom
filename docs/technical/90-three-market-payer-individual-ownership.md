## Problem and acceptance
A person comparing Individual, Support and Corporate entry routes must understand who might fund a membership versus who **owns** it. Approved TRN-BIZ-029 states that an individual always owns membership and personal healthcare wallet, even when a validly contracted organization or supportive institution pays subscription fees. Merely being a payer does not grant access to private healthcare or wallet, and a third-party payment route is not established for the public-individual market. No real partner contract, price, PSP, split-payer, consent, member, insurance or finance integration exists.

Enhance the existing real-source-driven local three-market React interface, not the backend and not a new static fabricated market registry. Once `GET /internal/ops/v1/market-entry` has passed its existing strict complete three-market DENIED validation, add an accessible interactive comparison of `INDIVIDUAL` vs `THIRD_PARTY` payer possibilities, using the server-provided `thirdPartyPayerContractRequired`, ownership and sponsor-privacy flags, preserving exact 3 routes and all BLOCKED statuses. Public is not presented as third-party option. Support and Corporate third-party possibility clearly require as-yet-unverified contracts. No prices, activated payment buttons, account forms, invoice, subsidy, actual contract or rights; labels are prospective only.

If the source becomes unavailable or any forged sponsor access/READY/active-onboarding flags fail validation, the comparison disappears instead of retaining stale action-oriented information. Browser Chromium must select both payer options, verify all three source-backed markets, individual ownership and sponsor clinical privacy, source loss and forged permission removal; the full 14 canonical Push and PR CI jobs (including PostgreSQL/Chromium/backup-recovery) are mandatory.

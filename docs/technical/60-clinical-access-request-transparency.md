# TRN-TECH-060 — Scoped clinical access request transparency, without record access

**Recorded:** 2026-10-11. **Stacked base:** Sprint50 PR #57, Draft/Open. **Business:** TRN-BIZ-029/035/036/041/046/047 (member controls need-to-know clinical record sharing, sponsor cannot see medical records, treatment access distinct from research/AI, emergency exception separately licensed), backlog TRN-EP-01/02/06/10/13.

## Distinct user problem
Sprint42 introduced an INTERNAL clinical-record disclosure *precondition inspector* and a disconnected record-sharing view. It did **not** let a member even understand whether a clinician's request, a member concern and subsequent independent oversight are the *same* source-bound process. Sprint51 introduces a separate **claim-only technical paper trail**, not a live activity audit, real access history, EHR record or consent backend.

## Composition, privacy, authority
`SyntheticClinicalAccessTransparencyTrail` uses `SyntheticClinicalRecordDisclosureGate.Assess` for exact bounded `test:` treatment-purpose claims. A scope pins precise tenant/member, clinician, category, service, purpose, data-source revision, member-control revision, clinical-need revision and every independent untrusted EvidenceCheck. Open request is only a *paper record of a claimed clinician request*, not a read or professional licence. A claimed member concern (separate actor marker matching exact member) and an unverified external oversight note (a marker distinct from clinician and member) are append-only, UUID-idempotent, CAS/versioned and source-pinned. A change of entire claimed scope, denial status, category, source, member, service, clinician or request key after opening is refused. One note of each kind in this initial narrow synthetic trace; **no real legal complaint lifecycle or approved resolution is defined**. A negative member choice can remain noted as denied in the request, never a grant. Sponsor/employer/coordinator, research/AI and legal-emergency purpose are NOT treatment-clinician request types here and must use separate future approved contracts.

`ProjectForClaimedMember` independently invokes the already existing `MemberSelfReadBoundary.Assess` for the member's **separate personal read** prerequisites, plus exact matched tenant/member scope, before showing *even claimed paper counts*. Unknown/revoked access/invalid hints or sponsor role fail closed without a case title or metadata. Projection includes only counts (one unverified request, concern/oversight note counts), revision and untrusted assessor category. **No raw claimed clinician identifier, medical record, category, clinical note, document, diagnosis, patient data or source URL** is projected. Crucially `RecordReadOccurred=false`, `DisclosureGranted=false`, `LegalExceptionEstablished=false` always. All checks are supplied by synthetic fixture and neither prove authentication nor independently auditable activity.

## UI and testing
Extend existing RTL `/member/records` to distinguish (1) clinician's scoped *claimed request*, (2) member's independent right to challenge/inquire, (3) independent accountability oversight; all unknown and controls disabled. Existing personal self-read, research consent separation and emergency help remain unchanged. Add four built Chromium regressions for no fake accesses, sponsor denial, source privacy, no network/storage and navigation/focus. Add adverse xUnit for source mutation, exact UUID collisions, stale/duplicate notes, request vs challenge, denial, scope mismatch and member-only projection; inherit all 12 CI jobs including isolated real PostgreSQL and DENY-only external AI/national launch checks.

## Operational blockers
Real clinical identities/credential checks, actual patient consent/withdrawal, immutable independent access logs and regulated audit source, member-authenticated challenge and evidence capture, lawful notice/time rules and human investigation, legal emergency exceptions, interoperable clinical records and secure storage/network access, privacy/regulatory/care review, national scale are all absent. **No real disclosure/access**, no Production and no national release. All PRs Draft/Open/Unmerged; no Ready/Merge/Stage/QA Gate/Release Approval/Production without user authorization.

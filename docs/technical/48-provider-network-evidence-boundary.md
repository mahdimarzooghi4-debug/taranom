# TRN-TECH-048 — Distinct provider and network qualification evidence without financial or clinical activation

**Date:** 2026-10-11. Stacked on Sprint38 Draft PR #45. Accepted policies: TRN-BIZ-043 (direct providers and licensed partner networks), TRN-BIZ-044 (Taranom establishes/oversees uniform standards; qualified networks control own providers only within lawful contracts; each provider retains independent service-specific competence), TRN-BIZ-009 (credit is paid only to a legally eligible recipient directly contracted with Taranom), TRN-BIZ-035/046 (personal clinical permissions and clinical decisions remain separate).

## Design and explicit safety

No real licensed provider, professional board, legal entity, provider/network contract, roster, clinical case, payee, Taranom payment authorization or national network exists. Rather than inventing a license validation authority or admitting fictional actors, Sprint39 adds an INTERNAL test-only **pure, read-only** `SyntheticProviderNetworkEvidenceGate` in the already independent `Taranom.ClinicalCenterGovernance` bounded assembly. The existing Center manager/affiliation workflow remains untouched: center affiliation never equates to actual qualification.

- Exactly two distinct `SyntheticProviderPath` labels: `DirectContract` and `QualifiedPartnerNetwork`. No network claim may be smuggled into a direct-provider inspection. The partner-network path must identify a synthetic partner and its precise proposed member-provider association.
- Exact opaque `test:` tenant, proposed provider, service-scope and claimed clinical-responsibility provider; mismatched identity or non-test references fail closed. No real patient/member/provider IDs, license number, fee, rates, contract dates, location or clinical health data are stored.
- Four independently required **caller-claimed** provider checks: current professional qualification, service-specific scope, distinct responsibility evidence, current provider-quality oversight. Network path additionally requires three independent check categories: network's own qualification, network contract/delegation, and actual provider control.
- Separate `InspectProviderQualification` and `InspectProposedTreatmentCreditRecipient` purposes. Even an apparently qualified network NEVER substitutes for a direct Taranom contract with the **proposed legal payment recipient**. Exact `ProposedPayeeRef` must equal `ClaimedDirectTaranomContractPayeeRef`, and `DirectTaranomPayeeContractCurrent` evidence must be separately `Confirmed`. No assumption that the legal payee and the individual treating professional must be the same actor — a licensed center may be a separate legal recipient. The gate also does not assert eligibility or settle a real payment.
- Missing/unknown future evidence fail closed, explicit rejection dominates unknown, wrong network or payee scope fails. Even ALL caller-claimed checks `Confirmed` return ONLY `PreconditionsPresentOnly`: never real licensure, actual provider-network enrollment, authorized clinical treatment, real data/record access, credit transfer, claim, settlement or national coverage.
- No generic public CRUD or provider registry API, storage, provider payment execution, AI service, model selection, or invented criterion/rating is created.

## Existing Center browser experience

`/center/qualifications` shows TWO distinct descriptive cards with no verified records: direct contract, and regulated partner-network arrangement. It explicitly separates network-level legal qualification and delegated provider control, Taranom oversight, individual provider qualification, service-specific responsibility, and independent Taranom contract for each proposed medical-credit payment recipient. A control for activating the network remains DISABLED; no clinician, network, contract, service or payout reference is shown as existing. All other Center UI sections, RTL, WCAG controls, CSP `connect-src 'none'`, anti-frame HTTP headers and source bundle SHA-256 controls remain.

## Problem and scope

The existing `ProofingCaseJournal` and `PgProofingCaseJournal` implement the important independent, immutable synthetic sequence:
1. a source-claimed definitive mismatch from otherwise synthetically validated references → `CorrectApplicantInformationThenReverify`;
2. an applicant *declares* correction while official-activation status is separately **claimed** known and inactive → increment identityRevision and reset review to `AwaitingAuthoritativeVerification`;
3. any new proofing observation must pin the new identityRevision, and even a claimed matching result creates only `ValidatedProofingEvidenceOnly`, **never** membership activation, 90-day waiting clock, wallet credit, member identity, national identifier or real IdP request.

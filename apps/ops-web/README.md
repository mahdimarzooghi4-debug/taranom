# Taranom Operations Web

The existing `/operations/*` pages remain unchanged. Two additional frontend-only Figma panel previews are available at `/admin/*` and `/support-institution/*`.

- `/admin`: dashboard, users, centers, appointments, prescriptions, financial reports, support institutions, four Taranom fund cards, and access levels.
- `/support-institution`: dashboard, beneficiaries, programs, beneficiary setup, case workflow, budget allocation, payment recording, transactions, financial reporting, Taranom fund support, charity fund, organization profile, and user setup.

All records and amounts in these two previews are synthetic. Buttons and forms do not save or send requests. The screens do not establish identity, role permissions, fund ownership, beneficiary eligibility, actual budgets or payments, or access to clinical records. Backend contracts will be handled separately.
